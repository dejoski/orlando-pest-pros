# Organic search launch checks

This is an independent quote-request site, not a pest-control operator. No storefront address, local-business listing, invented reviews, or technician credentials are required or claimed. Structured data describes the website organization and quote-request services. Schema does not guarantee rich results or rankings.

## Before expecting a real lead

- Deploy the reviewed build to the production domain and verify HTTPS, a 200 home page, redirect consistency, rendered canonicals, robots.txt, and sitemap-index.xml. Keep the production origin synchronized in src/config.ts, astro.config.mjs, and public/robots.txt.
- Configure durable lead storage and notification delivery. Submit one clearly labeled test inquiry and verify both the stored record and delivery. Confirm attribution arrives with the successful lead. A phone-link click is not a completed call.
- Set up an actual provider or accountable lead recipient. A saved inquiry is not a service appointment; response time, licensing, pricing, and guarantees must be confirmed by the provider.
- Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console, submit the sitemap, and inspect the home page plus priority city/service pages. Verification tokens must come from the owner’s account; never invent them.
- Check impressions, indexing exclusions, search clicks, and successful organic-attributed form submissions separately. Blank dashboards do not establish zero rankings. Rankings and first-lead timing cannot be guaranteed.
- Review the first priority pages for specific, useful local pest guidance. Expand city/service pages only when their content serves a distinct local question; shared templates and sitemap priority values do not establish ranking quality.
- Build legitimate local references and links where relevant. Do not create a fake Google Business Profile or use a borrowed address.

## Technical coverage

Public service and location pages have canonicals, internal links, breadcrumbs, visible FAQs, and sitemap discovery. Thank-you and private dashboard pages are excluded from the sitemap and marked noindex. The thank-you page cannot itself prove a successful form submission; conversions must be recorded after server-confirmed storage.

## External blockers

Live deployment, domain ownership, Google indexing, recipient/provider setup, configured secrets, and real search traffic must be verified outside a source-code review. Time alone does not resolve these blockers. Once checked, organic acquisition still depends on relevance, competition, links, and search demand.
